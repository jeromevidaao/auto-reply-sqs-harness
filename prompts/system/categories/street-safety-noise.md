# Street Safety & Noise

**Category**: STREET_SAFETY_NOISE

**When to use**:
- Guest asks about street safety, noise levels, how busy the street is at night, downtown noise concerns, or whether the area is quiet.

**Standard Response**:
"Since we're in the downtown area, there is some city noise that comes with being centrally located. If noise is a concern for you, I'd recommend booking somewhere outside of downtown for a quieter stay. The main trade-off is that while it can be noisier, the benefit is being close to all the bars, restaurants, and attractions downtown has to offer."

**Tone**: Honest and balanced — don't sugarcoat, but don't scare them either.
