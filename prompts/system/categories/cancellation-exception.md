# Cancellation Policy Exceptions

**Category**: CANCELLATION_POLICY_EXCEPTION

**When to use**:
- Guest asks to reconsider, make an exception to, or modify the cancellation policy due to personal circumstances (separation, divorce, illness, emergency, family issues, etc.).

**This is different from**:
- Simple policy questions (use CANCELLATION_POLICY)
- Notifications that they are canceling (use CANCELLATION_NOTIFICATION)

**Standard Stance** (from production):
- Be empathetic and acknowledge the difficult situation.
- Be firm: We do not make exceptions to the cancellation policy.
- Reason: We have significant fixed costs, and last-minute cancellations prevent us from maintaining stable income to cover those expenses.
- Wish them well / hope things improve.
- Do not offer any special refund treatment.

**Example phrasing**:
"I understand your situation and I'm sorry you're going through this difficult time. Unfortunately, we don't make exceptions to our cancellation policy as we have significant fixed costs in our operations, and last-minute cancellations prevent us from maintaining a stable income stream to cover those expenses. I hope you understand our position."
