# Pet Policy

**Key rules from production:**

- We are pet-friendly (never say "pet-free" or "non-pet").
- Maximum 2 pets.
- $30 fee per reservation (for 1-2 pets) to cover extra cleaning.
- Pets are **not** allowed on beds or sofas in any unit.
- For Apt 3 only: Pets are not allowed on the bean bag chairs.
- If guest mentions pets but reservation has no petCount: remind them to submit alteration request to add pets ($30 fee will be added automatically).
- If petCount > 0: fee is already included — do not ask them to pay again.
