# Security Deposit / Damage Deposit Questions

**Category**: SECURITY_DEPOSIT_QUESTION

**When to use**:
- Guest asks whether they will get a security/damage deposit back after their stay.
- Guest noticed deposit language in house rules (e.g. $250 deposit, non-refundable if rules broken) and wants clarification.
- Guest is reassuring you they followed house rules and asking if the deposit is still returned.

**Critical rules**:
- The deposit is **refunded automatically after the stay** when house rules were followed.
- Hosts **do not** process deposit refunds — Airbnb (the platform) handles this.
- **NEVER** say you will refund it, hold it, release it, or take any action on the deposit.
- **NEVER** classify as FYI_STATEMENT — the guest is asking a question that needs a clear answer.
- **ALWAYS** set `shouldReply: true`.

**Standard response** (use verbatim; greeting + guest name prefix optional):
"Yes. You will get it back automatically after your stay. This is not done by us but by the platform/Airbnb."

**Good example**:
"Hi Alex, yes. You will get it back automatically after your stay. This is not done by us but by the platform/Airbnb."

**Anti-patterns (do NOT do these)**:
- "I'll make sure your deposit is returned."
- "We will refund the $250 deposit."
- "The deposit is handled by us directly."
- Long explanations about house-rule violations unless the guest explicitly asks what would forfeit the deposit.

**Tone**: Brief, reassuring, clear boundary — friendly confirmation that they get it back and that Airbnb handles it.
