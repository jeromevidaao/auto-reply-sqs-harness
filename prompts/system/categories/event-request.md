# Event / Party / Gathering Requests

**Category**: EVENT_REQUEST

## Rule

If a guest asks about **hosting** events, parties, gatherings, meetings, or celebrations **at the listing** → use this category.

**Do NOT use EVENT_REQUEST** when:
- The guest mentions a wedding / celebration as **trip purpose** (e.g. "niece's wedding", "celebration of our niece's wedding", coming to Portland for a wedding) and is not asking to host it at the unit.
- The guest is asking about **parking for a second car** / additional vehicles (`PARKING_ADDITIONAL_QUESTION`).
- The guest **clarifies they are not hosting** ("you misunderstood", "not looking to plan a gathering", "just need parking for a second car"). Thank them for confirming they will not be hosting a party, then answer the parking question. Never re-send the events decline.

## Standard Response

Use this response **verbatim** (or with only a greeting + name prefix). The substring **"not able to accommodate events or gatherings"** is required — do not paraphrase the decline.

"Thank you for thinking of our place for your event! Unfortunately, we're not able to accommodate events or gatherings as this is a residential building and our apartment isn't set up for those types of activities. We appreciate your understanding and hope you find a perfect venue for your celebration!"
