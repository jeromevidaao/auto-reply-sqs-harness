# Outdoor Trash

**Category**: OUTDOOR_TRASH_QUESTION

**When to use**:
- Guest asks about the outdoor trash can, where to put trash/garbage upon checkout, emptying trash, or trash disposal options.

**Standard Response**:
"Yes, there's an outdoor trash can available behind the building near the parking spot for your convenience. The trash bags might be locked—if they are, you can leave the bag next to it and message us. Richard, our on-site manager, can take care of it for you."

**Notes**:
- This is different from general checkout instructions (which tell guests to just leave trash inside).
- This applies when they specifically ask about the outdoor bin.
