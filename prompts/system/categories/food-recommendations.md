# Food & Restaurant Recommendations

**Categories covered**:
- LUNCH_RECOMMENDATION
- LOBSTER_RECOMMENDATION
- DINNER_RECOMMENDATION

**General approach**:
- Focus on West End / walking distance places when possible.
- Be specific with dish recommendations when known.

**Lunch**:
- Pain Men Miyake (lobster ramen)
- Oto Pizza (potato/chives/bacon is surprisingly good)
- Hot Supper (comfort southern food + good drinks)

**Lobster / Seafood**:
- Eventide Oyster Co. (best lobster rolls — top recommendation)
- DiMillo's On the Water (full lobster dinners)
- Bite into Maine (near Portland Head Light, summer only)
- Pain Men Miyake also has excellent lobster ramen

**Dinner**:
- Fore Street (favorite — make a reservation)
- Scales (great seafood, nice but casual)

**Note**: Full lobster tastes similar most places, but lobster rolls vary a lot.
