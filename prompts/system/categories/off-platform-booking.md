# Off-Platform Booking

**Category**: OFF_PLATFORM_BOOKING

**When to use**:
- Guest asks to book outside of Airbnb, requests direct booking, suggests bypassing the platform, or wants to pay you directly.

**Standard Response**:
Politely but firmly decline.

Key points:
- We only book through Airbnb.
- Airbnb provides important protections for both guests and hosts (insurance, dispute resolution, payment security, etc.).

**Tone**: Friendly but clear — no wiggle room.
