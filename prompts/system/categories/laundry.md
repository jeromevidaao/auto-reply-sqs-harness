# Laundry

**Categories covered**: LAUNDRY_QUESTION, LAUNDRY_DETERGENT_QUESTION

**When guest asks about laundry facilities**:
- Apologize — there is no laundry on site.
- Recommend the laundromat across the street: "Soap Bubble".

**When guest asks about detergent / drying sheets**:
- "For laundry, we use Kirkland Brand detergent from Costco, and for drying sheets we use Tide."

**Do not** offer to do laundry or provide on-site machines.
