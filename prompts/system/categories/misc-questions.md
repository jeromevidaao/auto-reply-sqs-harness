**FLOOR_STAIRS_QUESTION**:

**BATH_AMENITIES_QUESTION**:
- Yes, we provide bath towels, soap, and shampoo.

**MARINA_DOWNTOWN_PARKING**:
- The marina is about a 20-minute walk — a nice walk.
- Recommend walking or Uber instead of driving/parking downtown (can be difficult and expensive).
- Suggest SpotHero for paid parking if needed.

**SOFA_BED_SIZE** (all units with a sofa bed):
- The queen size sofa bed comfortably sleeps 2.
- Linens and pillows are stored in the storage compartment under the sofa (in the sofa itself).
- When the guest asks about sheets/blankets/pillows for the couch/sofa bed, confirm we provide them and include the storage location — do not reply with only "yes, we provide linens" without mentioning they are under/in the sofa.

**PACK_AND_PLAY_BRAND**:
- Use this specific category whenever the guest directly asks about crib / Pack and Play / portable crib / baby bed availability — even if the message is the very first post-booking communication from the guest and includes birthday/celebration intro language.
- After the greeting+name and comma, continue naturally with a lowercase or fitting start per the golden example: e.g. the substance should contain "yes we use the Graco Pack and Play and it's already set up and ready in the unit for you" (or very close variation that includes "Graco Pack and Play", "already", "ready", "set up"). Do not start the fact with a capital letter that makes it look like a new sentence if it breaks flow after the comma.
- We use the Graco Pack and Play. It is already set up and ready to use in the unit — no need to request or prepare anything special. All units come with one pre-placed.
- Never say "happy to have one ready", "we can get one ready", "upon request", or imply the guest needs to ask us to prepare/bring it. It is already present for guests.
- When this category applies on a first-host-message, still follow any dynamic GREETING INSTRUCTIONS (time-based greeting + natural guest name at the start). Do not let general welcome rules override this specific category.

**COOKING_UTENSILS**:
