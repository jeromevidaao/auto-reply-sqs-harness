# Golden: Payment Method Update (Julie AMEX)

**Scenario**: Guest updates payment method after fraudulent charges on prior Visa; asks host to bill AMEX instead.

**Approved ideal behavior**:
- Direct guest to Airbnb for payment/card changes.
- Do not offer to note, update, or charge a specific card.

**Rubric requirements**:
- Category: PAYMENT_METHOD_UPDATE
- Must mention Airbnb and that hosts do not handle payments
- Must NOT say "I'll note" or promise to bill AMEX
